# Design Context — Spell Survivors

## Product
A dark-fantasy roguelite survival game playable in the browser, primarily on mobile (landscape orientation). The player auto-attacks enemies, levels up, and sockets magical gems into a wand to build a power fantasy.

## Target Audience
Casual-to-mid-core mobile gamers who enjoy fantasy themes, fast sessions (5–15 min), and the dopamine loop of gear upgrades. Ages 16–35. Expect bold, legible UI at arm's length on a phone screen.

## Brand Personality
**Dungeon Naturalism.** Think torchlit stone corridors, mossy dungeon walls, cracked earth floors, ancient carved runes, worn leather, and wood beams. Warm amber torchfire casts deep shadows over rough stone. Evokes: classic tabletop dungeon crawlers, Dark Souls item descriptions, Diablo II inventory textures. Atmospheric and grounded — "you are a weathered wanderer in a real dungeon, not a glowing wizard rave."

## Tone
Earthy, weighty, worn. The magic is *real* but subtle — it's in the staff orb and the glowing eye, not plastered everywhere. Stone surfaces feel carved, not printed. Torchlight is warm and flickering, not neon. The world has texture, grime, and history.

## Design Tokens
- Torch orange (primary accent): `#c07020` / bright: `#e09030`
- Torch dim: `rgba(192,112,32,0.35)`
- Dungeon stone background: `#120d08` (warm dark brown-black, NOT void-black)
- Stone surface: `#1e1810` / `#2a2018`
- Moss green (relics): `#507840` / bright: `#70a050`
- Blood crimson (HP): `#8a1010` / bright: `#cc2020`
- Rarity common: `#607090` (pewter/lead)
- Rarity rare: `#3a70c0` (sapphire)
- Rarity legendary: `#c07820` (ember amber)
- Fonts: Georgia serif for titles, monospace for stats

## Player Character
Grey-cloaked wizard. Worn charcoal-grey wool cloak (`#484840`), dark hem (`#282820`), earth-brown leather belt (`#6a3e14`), torch-amber buckle. Dark charcoal hat. The ONLY magic-coloured elements are the staff's arcane purple orb and the single glowing eye — grey on the outside, power on the inside.

## Key UI Surfaces
1. **Start screen** — torch-warm dungeon entrance, warm amber radial glow
2. **HUD** (in-game) — XP bar (torch orange fill), HP bar (blood red), level badge (stone-carved)
3. **Inventory panel** — dark stone chamber with torch-amber borders
4. **Upgrade draft screen** — choose 1 of 3 gem cards on level-up (stagger-deal entrance)
5. **Game over screen** — death summary with stats
6. **Forge result modal** — animated reveal of a newly forged gem

## Performance Constraints
- All game sprites are drawn procedurally on canvas (no image files)
- CSS animations must be GPU-friendly (transform + opacity only)
- Targets 60fps on mid-range Android phones
