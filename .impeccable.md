# Design Context — Spell Survivors

## Product
A dark-fantasy roguelite survival game playable in the browser, primarily on mobile (landscape orientation). The player auto-attacks enemies, levels up, and sockets magical gems into a wand to build a power fantasy.

## Target Audience
Casual-to-mid-core mobile gamers who enjoy fantasy themes, fast sessions (5–15 min), and the dopamine loop of gear upgrades. Ages 16–35. Expect bold, legible UI at arm's length on a phone screen.

## Brand Personality
**Dark arcane fantasy.** Think crumbling dungeons, ancient runes, alchemical gold, void magic. Evokes: Diablo II inventory screen, dark fantasy grimoires, tabletop RPG equipment sheets. Serious and atmospheric but not grim—there's power fantasy here. "You are a powerful mage and these enemies are beneath you."

## Tone
Dramatic, weighty, arcane. Gold and blood-crimson accents. Never casual or bright. Typography should feel carved in stone or inked in a spellbook.

## Design Tokens
- Gold: `#c89840` / bright `#f0c050`
- Crimson: `#c01828`
- Arcane teal: `#20b898`
- Purple: `#b060ff`
- Background: near-black `#08060e`
- Fonts: Georgia serif for titles, monospace for data/stats

## Key UI Surfaces
1. **Start screen** — atmospheric first impression, sets tone
2. **HUD** (in-game) — XP bar, HP bar, level badge, timer, kill counter, wand button, relic slots
3. **Inventory panel** — wand sockets + 9-slot gem storage, drag-and-drop
4. **Upgrade draft screen** — choose 1 of 3 cards on level-up
5. **Game over screen** — death summary with stats
6. **Forge result modal** — animated reveal of a newly forged gem

## Performance Constraints
- All game sprites are drawn procedurally on canvas (no image files)
- CSS animations must be GPU-friendly (transform + opacity only)
- Targets 60fps on mid-range Android phones
